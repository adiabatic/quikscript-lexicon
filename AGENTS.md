# Quikscript Lexicon

- All repo prose — docs, docstrings, comments, test names, and user-visible Desk strings — uses American spellings ("spelled", "recognized", "color").
- British spellings stay where they are data or quotation: verbatim quotes from the Quikscript Manual or other sources, corpus headwords and lexicon entries (the ReadLex corpus is British English), rule slugs and IDs that name data, and license texts.
